02An unconventional path

Different disciplines. One way of thinking.

I'm an Associate Director and Senior Forward Deployed Engineer at Vista Equity Partners. Before that I spent four years consulting at Slalom, on work that ran from discovery and architecture through implementation, security, release engineering, and the handoff to the people who operate it.

Before software, I spent more than a decade as a professional poker player and won a World Series of Poker bracelet. That experience still shapes how I work: model the system, question the assumptions, and make the best decision the evidence supports.
