An unconventional path

Different disciplines.
One way of thinking.

I'm a full stack consultant at Slalom, working across agentic delivery, developer platforms, and enterprise software. My work runs from discovery and architecture through implementation, security, release engineering, and the handoff to people who will operate it.

Before software, I spent more than a decade as a professional poker player and won a World Series of Poker bracelet. That experience still shapes how I work: model the system, question the assumptions, and make the best decision the evidence supports.
